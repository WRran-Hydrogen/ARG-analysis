### 2.3 Strict Clan Bitscore Assignment, Own-Seed FIMO, Coverage Filter & Tagged Prophages
1. **Strict Clan Highest-Bitscore Assignment (`resolve_strict_cl0013`)**:
   - Within overlapping `Pfam-A --cut_ga` domain clusters (`ov >= 30 aa`), sequences are assigned **strictly to the `CL0013` family with highest domain bitscore**, resolving the artificial `PF13354`/`PF00768` boundary conflict (`dual_PF13354_PF00768_GA` remains logged).
   - Previously, `764` viral `PF00768`-highest-scoring proteins (incl. `294 / 295` FIMO-rejected candidates carrying `SXXK` with median length `237 aa`) were misassigned to `PF13354`; under strict Clan bitscore, viral GA survivors cleanly split into **`PF00144 = 2,105`**, **`PF00905 = 1,331`**, **`PF13354 = 261`**, and sister PBP **`PF00768 = 895`**.
2. **Symmetric Own-Family Official-Seed `FIMO 5.5.9 (p <= 1e-5)`**:
   - Every sequence is filtered exclusively against its **own assigned family's** official Pfam seed MEME model across domain slices (`env_from..env_to ± 10 aa`), completely eliminating the prior `71% vs 88%` asymmetry:
     - `PF00144`: Viral `2,086 / 2,105 (99.10%)` | Bacterial `13,225 / 13,312 (99.35%)`
     - `PF00905`: Viral `1,323 / 1,331 (99.40%)` | Bacterial `16,080 / 16,203 (99.24%)`
     - `PF13354`: Viral **`260 / 261 (99.62%)`** | Bacterial `3,386 / 3,456 (97.97%)`
     - `PF00768`: Viral **`895 / 895 (100.00%)`** | Bacterial `3,889 / 3,896 (99.82%)`
     - `PF02113`: Bacterial `1,990 / 2,014 (98.81%)` (Viral `0`)
3. **HMM Coverage Filter (`hmm_cov >= 0.65` Primary; `>=0.60` / `>=0.70` Tracked)**:
   - `hmm_cov` = number of HMM match states covered by the winning family's domain / HMM `LENG`. hmmsearch can report one domain as several hits of the same family (a divergent, poorly scoring middle region, or a genuine internal insertion), so **co-linear same-family hits are merged** (no ≥30 aa overlap in the protein, same order in protein and HMM, each piece adding ≥50% new match states; candidates only from the winning hit's own envelope cluster or from clusters the same family wins, never from regions won by another family) and coverage is the union of their HMM intervals; the domain slice spans all pieces (insertions become lowercase insert states in hmmalign). A real internal deletion still lowers coverage. `hmm_cov_best_piece` and `n_domain_pieces` are kept per sequence.
   - Merging rescued `131` viral (`PF00768 116`, `PF00144 9`, `PF00905 3`, `PF13354 3`; median merged coverage `0.84`) and `461` bacterial sequences (`PF00905 279`, `PF13354 141`, `PF00144 27`, `PF00768 14`); multi-piece domains still `< 0.65`: `6` viral, `6` bacterial.
   - Viral sequences passing FIMO & `hmm_cov >= 0.65`: `PF00144 1,876`; `PF00905 1,242`; `PF13354 248`; `PF00768 837`.
4. **Triple-Source Bacterial Pool with Tagged Prophages (`in_bacterial_prophage`)**:
   - Combines `3,000` phylogenetically balanced GTDB/RefSeq representatives (`46` phyla, `837` families; `99.80%` 1:1 native CDS), `259` dereplicated Marine MAGs (`100%` 1:1 CDS), and `1,219` `CARD/SARG` clinical gold standards.
   - Loci inside geNomad provirus intervals are **retained with `in_bacterial_prophage = True` (`replicon = prophage`)** for paired `with_prophage` (Primary) vs. `without_prophage` (Sensitivity) testing.
   - Whole contigs called viral by geNomad inside a GTDB assembly or Marine MAG (most likely free phage genomes binned with the host, not integrated prophages) are tagged **`replicon = viral_contig` / `is_viral_contig_in_genome = True`**, kept in the catalog, and **excluded from every bacterial tree, HyPhy and outgroup set**.

All `23` PF00905 events have the same recipients under either root. Sister-PRE (appendix), crown projection, clean 12-sequence GTDB outgroups, MinVar and Midpoint are reported as comparators.
   - **Tree Support & HGT Inference (`IQ-TREE 2.4.0`, `-m MFP -mset LG,WAG,Q.pfam,VT,JTT -B 1000 --bnni -alrt 1000`)**:
     - Double-outgroup topological nesting (Irwin et al. 2022) + **concordant two-pass Fitch parsimony AND `PastML` marginal ML ancestral state reconstruction**;
     - Strict **`Tier-1 HGT`**: `UFBoot >= 95%` AND `SH-aLRT >= 80%` on **`min(P, G)`**;

The pre-registered classification is kept as `is_tier1_hgt_prereg`.
     - **Single-tip events** are reported separately, and every pre-registered Tier-1 event is tested with an **AU test** (IQ-TREE `-z ... -au -zb 10000`) of the ML tree against the best tree in which the focal tips are not nested in the donor background (constrained search, `scripts/hpc/04_hgt_inference/03`–`05`). Model parameters for the test are fitted on the ML tree (`-te`); a constrained tree with a higher likelihood than the ML tree (in the test or as the score of its own search) means the unconstrained search missed a better tree, and the event is reported as `ml_tree_suboptimal` (nesting not supported).
     - **Long-branch check (reported, not filtered)**: for every single-tip event the recipient's terminal branch length, its percentile among all terminal branches of the tree and its ratio to the median terminal branch of the S1 + S2 background are reported; events at or above the 95th percentile are flagged (`long_branch_flag`), because a single nested tip on a long branch is also the pattern of long-branch attraction, contamination or a misassembled locus.
     - **Detection scope**: the criteria above (focal clade `< 25 %` of tips and `< 50 %` of the recipient-domain tips, purity `>= 0.80`, donor background `>= 0.75`, parent and grandparent UFBoot `>= 95`, no event on the two root branches) are designed for, and only have power for, **recent transfers**. Deep transfers are listed in `All_Trees_HGT_Events_Long.tsv` but cannot reach Tier 1: deep nodes of these trees are weakly supported (`1,563–1,916` tips, `239–329` columns, about three quarters of the tree length on terminal branches), family root positions are unresolved, the binary Bacterial/Viral ASR at deep nodes depends on the sampling ratio (`1,500` bacterial vs `63–416` viral tips), and later transfers or prophage integration erode purity. The absence of ancient Tier-1 events is therefore not evidence that ancient transfers did not occur; ancient transfers are analysed separately (Section 4.3: backbone trees, donor-lineage and host-concordance tests).

Scripts `scripts/hpc/04_hgt_inference/06`–`11` (driver `09_submit_ancient_hgt.sh`); outputs in `$SBL_HPC_BASE/04_hgt_inference/ancient_hgt/`.

1. **Host and bacterial lineages (`06`)**: viral hosts are the IMG/VR host predictions (no new prediction), read from the IMG/VR host- or sequence-information table (path in `SBL_HPC_IMGVR_HOST_TABLE`). Several predictions for one UViG are merged to the deepest rank on which they all agree; isolate-genome hosts override the other methods. Bacterial lineages are GTDB (reference genomes) and GTDB-Tk (marine MAGs); clinical CARD/SARG sequences have an NCBI phylum only. Older GTDB/NCBI names are mapped to current GTDB names (e.g. Firmicutes → Bacillota, Proteobacteria → Pseudomonadota, Betaproteobacteria → Gammaproteobacteria). A viral tip's host is the consensus (`>= 70 %`) of its `nr85` cluster.
2. **Backbone trees (`07`–`08`)**: one reduced tree per PRE family, built to keep sequence and taxonomic breadth with about `420` tips, so that a site-heterogeneous model is affordable. Viral: every hydrolytic SBL and Tier-1/Tier-2 recipient, then one sequence per MMseqs2 50 %-identity cluster (largest first; within a cluster, hosts known first, then viral-origin evidence Tier 1 > Tier 2 and MOSAiC > Tier 3), up to `140`; then, outside that budget, every viral member of every deep viral clade of the primary trees (revision, item 8). Bacterial (`260`, GTDB lineage to class, HMM coverage `>= 0.8`): the closest bacterial hit of every sampled viral sequence (so candidate donor lineages are present), Tier-1/Tier-2 recipients, one sequence per phylum, then one per 50 % cluster choosing the least-sampled order. All `24` Sister-PRE outgroup sequences. Alignment on the FoldMason 3D grid of the `CL0013` crystal anchors (as for the structure-guided `CL0013` and joint trees) + ClipKIT gappy 0.5. Trees: LG+F+G guide → **LG+C60+F+G PMSF** with UFBoot/SH-aLRT 1000, and a second PMSF run with **100 nonparametric bootstrap replicates summarised as transfer bootstrap expectation (TBE)**, the support measure suited to deep nodes.
3. **Deep clades (`10`)**: on the primary trees (roots MAD [reference], MinVar, Sister-PRE outgroup, clean GTDB outgroup) and the backbone trees (roots Sister-PRE outgroup [reference], MAD, MinVar), every maximal supported (UFBoot `>= 95`, SH-aLRT `>= 80`) viral clade (viral purity `>= 0.80`, `>= 3` viral tips) and every maximal supported bacterial clade (`>= 2` tips) nested in a viral background is listed. A clade is **deep** when it exceeds a Tier-1 size cap, is a child of the root, or its own diversification (mean node-to-tip distance) is `>= 0.5` of the tree radius, defined as half the median patristic distance between ingroup tips (root-free; revision, item 8). Direction is called per root with the Tier-1 nesting rule (nested in the other domain, sister and aunt each contain the donor domain, background `>= 75 %` donor); a call is **root-invariant** when the same tip set is a clade with the same direction under every root.
4. **Donor lineage and host concordance (`10`)**: the donor lineage of a viral clade is the consensus GTDB phylum/class/order (`>= 70 %`) of the bacterial tips of its sister subtree (aunt subtree when the sister holds no bacteria). Concordance with the clade's IMG/VR host lineage indicates capture from (or retention in) the lineage the viruses infect; discordance indicates capture from another lineage or a later host switch. Significance: host labels are permuted among the labelled viral tips of each tree (`10,000` permutations), for all viral clades and for deep clades, per family and pooled with family strata. Every host test is run twice: with all IMG/VR predictions and without `Isolate taxonomy` hosts, because a provirus in an isolate genome is assigned that genome as its host, which is partly circular for a comparison with its bacterial neighbours (IMG/VR v4.1, release 2022-12-19, `IMGVR_all_Host_information-high_confidence.tsv`: host predictions for `920` of `4,592` viral sequences, `559` of them from isolate taxonomy; PF13354 `167/261`, PF00144 `606/2,105`, PF00905 `125/1,331`, PF00768 `22/895`).
5. **Root-free host proximity (`10`)**: for every labelled viral tip, the majority lineage of its `3` nearest bacterial tips (patristic distance) is compared with its host, by distance quartile, with the same permutation. A host signal that persists in the far quartiles supports old host-derived capture; its loss with distance is expected if old transfers came from other lineages or hosts changed.
6. **AU tests (`10`, `11`; backbone, outgroup root, deep supported viral clades)**: *basal* — the bacterial ingroup forms a clade that excludes the viral clade (the viral lineage branches outside the bacterial crown); rejection means the viral lineage is nested within bacterial diversity, a direction call that does not rely on MAD/MinVar. *host* — when the host phylum differs from the donor phylum, the viral clade is constrained as sister to the nearest bacterial clade of its host phylum; rejection means capture from the host phylum is rejected. Constrained search and AU test under LG+C60+F+G with the backbone site frequencies (`-fs`), RELL `10,000` replicates.
7. **Limits**: donor assignment depends on which bacterial lineages are sampled (the backbone deliberately includes the nearest bacterial hits; agreement between primary and backbone trees is required before a donor is claimed); gene-tree paralogy (most genomes carry several PBP/PRE paralogs) means a donor lineage is a lineage of gene copies, not of genomes; IMG/VR host predictions cover only part of the UViGs and are mostly genus-level predictions with their own error rate; MOSAiC sequences (`25`) have no IMG/VR host prediction.
8. **One-time revision after the first backbone run (2026-11-25; declared before the rebuild, no further changes)**: the first backbone trees (version 1: `140` viral, `260` bacterial, `24` outgroup tips per family; LG+C60+F+G UFBoot finished, TBE not finished) contained no deep clade, so no AU test was run. Tracing the `16` deep clades of the primary trees into the backbone showed two problems with the design rather than with the data: (a) the 50 %-cluster sampling kept only `1–2` tips of `6` small deep clades (including the only root-invariant one, PF00144 `VC014`), so they could not be tested; (b) the depth ratio was measured against the median root-to-tip distance of the rooted tree, which depends on the root (backbone PF00144: `4.32` under the outgroup root, `2.99` under MinVar), so the two tree sets were not comparable. Two backbone clades reproduced primary deep clades as root-invariant B→V with donor lineage equal to host lineage (PF00144, Actinomycetes, `20` viral tips; PF00905, Bacteroidia, `28` viral tips), but their depth ratios (`0.36`, `0.29`) fell below `0.5`; the two largest primary deep clades (PF00905 `VC008`, `109` viral tips; PF00768 `VC001`, `158`) split into `3` and `7` smaller supported clades. Revision: every viral member of every deep viral clade of the primary trees is added to the backbone outside the `140`-sequence budget, and the depth denominator is the root-free tree radius for both tree sets and all roots; within a 50 % cluster the representative is now chosen by graded viral-origin evidence (Tier 1 > Tier 2 and MOSAiC > Tier 3; version 1 preferred Tier 1 only and ranked Tier 2 with Tier 3), using the viral-origin tiers in force when the backbone is rebuilt. Thresholds (depth ratio `0.5`, support, purity, size caps) are unchanged. Version-1 trees and tables are kept in `ancient_hgt/v1/` and are reported with the revised results.
9. **Within-clade selection on the deep viral clades (2026-11-25; declared before the run)**: the Stage 4A subclades (up to 4 per family, `8–55` sequences) cover only some deep viral clades, so retention under functional constraint was tested on every deep viral clade of the rebuilt backbone trees (outgroup root) with `>= 8` viral sequences that have a verified codon sequence. Only the viral members are used; a clade with more than `55` is subsampled at random to `55` (seed `20261125`). Codons are taken from the Stage 4A hmmalign-anchored codon alignment, and the tree is the Stage 4A rooted primary tree pruned to the selected tips. HyPhy FEL (default settings) is run per clade; purifying sites are β < α at `p <= 0.05`, positive sites β > α at `p <= 0.05`; the third-position saturation index (Xia's I_SS) is reported with each set (scripts `04_hgt_inference/12`–`13`; outputs `ancient_hgt/fel_deep/`, `Deep_Clade_FEL_Summary.tsv`).
10. **Results (2026-11-25; rebuilt backbone, rule-A viral-origin tiers; TBE support still running)**:
   - *Trees*: rebuilt backbones PF00144 `553` tips (`269` viral, `129` of them primary deep-clade members), PF00905 `499` (`215`, `75`), PF00768 `477` (`193`, `53`), PF13354 `347` (`63`, `0`); `223–304` columns, occupancy `0.86–0.94`, SXXK lock-in `0.92–0.98`. Deep viral clades: primary trees PF00144 `9`, PF00905 `3`, PF00768 `1`, joint tree `3`, PF13354 `0`; backbone trees PF00144 `6`, PF00905 `2`, PF00768 `2`, PF13354 `1`.
   - *Replication*: two lineages are root-invariant B→V in both tree sets, with donor lineage equal to host lineage: PF13354 (primary `VC003` = backbone `VC001`, `20` viral tips, Cyanobacteriia) and PF00905 (primary `VC001` = backbone `VC010`, `28` viral tips, Bacteroidia; depth ratio `0.34` in the backbone, below the deep threshold). Three further clades are root-invariant B→V in the backbone only (PF00144 `VC007`, `13` viral tips; PF00144 `VC009`, `37`; PF00905 `VC006`, `52` viral + `13` bacterial tips, donor Bacilli, host Clostridia). Not replicated: PF00144 `VC014` (root-invariant B→V in the primary trees) is a clade but not nested in the backbone; PF00768 `VC001` (`158` viral tips) splits into three clades that are not nested; PF00905 `VC003` is not a supported backbone clade.
   - *AU tests* (`12` tests): no *basal* test rejects a placement of the viral clade outside the bacterial crown (`11` clades, p-AU `0.17–0.59`; for `3` of them the constrained tree is more likely than the ML tree, `ml_tree_suboptimal`). The single *host* test rejects capture from the host phylum for PF00144 `VC014` (host Alphaproteobacteria; Pseudomonadota constraint, p-AU `0.012`, ΔlogL `120`). The position of deep viral clades relative to the bacterial crown is therefore unresolved, consistent with the unresolved family root positions (Section 4.2); B→V calls for deep clades are topological and are not supported against a basal placement.
   - *Host signal* (hosts without isolate taxonomy, class rank): the `3` nearest bacterial tips of a viral tip belong to its host class more often than under permutation in the backbone trees (PF00905 `1.00` vs `0.24` expected, PF13354 `1.00` vs `0.24`, PF00144 `0.73` vs `0.22`; p `<= 2e-4`) and in the primary trees (PF13354 `0.81` vs `0.21`, PF00905 `0.43` vs `0.14`, joint tree `0.35` vs `0.06`, p `1e-4`; PF00144 `0.23` vs `0.11`, p `0.053`). In the most distant quartile the signal remains for PF13354 in both tree sets (`4/4`, p `0.008`) and for PF00905 in the backbone (`6/6`, p `1e-4`), not in the PF00905 primary tree (`0/6`). Clade-level concordance of donor and host class, pooled over families: deep clades `2/3` in both tree sets (expected `0.01` backbone, `0.14` primary; p `<= 9e-4`). Quartiles hold only `3–6` tips each.
   - *Retention in viral lineages* (independent of the direction of the original transfer): the catalytic SXXK motif is present in `>= 97 %` of the members of every backbone deep viral clade, KTG in the PF00905 and PF00768 clades (KTG is not conserved in PF00144); all members are `PBP_or_PRE_Homolog` (no hydrolytic β-lactamase). FEL (`8` clades with `>= 8` sequences; `3` smaller clades skipped): purifying sites `36–91 %` of informative sites, positive sites `0–2` per clade, median site dN/dS `0.02–0.12`; the third codon positions are saturated in `7` of the `8` sets (I_SS above the symmetric critical value), so only the qualitative pattern (most sites under purifying selection, almost none under positive selection) is reported, and the one unsaturated set (PF00144 `VC007`, `13` sequences, `36 %` purifying, `1` positive site) shows the same pattern. Recombination was not tested on these sets (no GARD). Where IMG/VR v4.1 gives a topology (about half of the sequences), deep-clade members are mostly free viral genomes rather than proviruses (PF00144 `VC009` `0/17` proviruses, PF13354 `VC001` `3/13`, PF00144 `VC004` `7/43`; all viral SBL/PRE sequences `640/1,869`).
   - *Interpretation for reporting*: a bacterial origin of the viral SBL/PRE lineages is the most likely explanation (ancestral peptidoglycan transpeptidase function, recent B→V transfers supported by AU tests, host-proximity signal, two replicated lineages nested in their host lineages), but ancient transfer is not demonstrated by a phylogenetic test. Long-term retention under functional constraint within viral lineages is supported by the deep, well-supported viral-only clades, motif conservation and within-clade purifying selection. Version-1 results (no deep backbone clade, no AU test) are in `ancient_hgt/v1/`.
